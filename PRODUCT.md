## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated from the explicit request to begin work and the established neighboring
games: Next.js, React, and TypeScript. Ordinary chain integration and testing use
the shared loopback-only Tezos Localnet; Shadownet is reserved for an explicit
final-test rehearsal. Phase 0 is device-authoritative only for the player's
private prototype save and uses a deterministic reducer, so the first service
is not blocked on a wallet, contract, indexer, or public RPC and cannot be
presented as issuer-verified evidence.

## Users

Primary users enjoy short-session restaurant management, tactile systems, pixel
food art, and decisions whose consequences can be understood and improved. They
want the fantasy of running a profitable house, but they come to play—not to seek
financial return.

Secondary users are wallet-curious players and members of the wider Open Sign
Games community. They need optional ownership to be legible, deliberate, and
truthful about identity, fees, permanence, transferability, and finality.

Yield seekers and players primarily motivated by expected financial return are
not the design center.

## Product Purpose

The Tender Baron lets the player restore a faded, warm, storybook steakhouse by
conducting a serious fire and keeping a small dining room in harmony. Success
means coal placement, cut selection, resting, sides, and hospitality produce a
satisfying service before any wallet or token concept appears.

The game supports the fantasy of profit through restaurant stewardship: visible
revenue, ingredient and labor costs, waste, guest trust, and reinvestment in the
house. It does not present token appreciation, trading, or extraction as success.

## Positioning

Unlike a ticket-clearing restaurant reskin, The Tender Baron is a slow-burn
whole-room orchestration game. The player shapes a readable coal bed, moves each
cut through a deterministic heat path, uses resting as active planning time, and
lands the whole table together. A perfectly cooked steak can still miss the
occasion when its sides, pace, or hospitality are wrong.

## Operating Context

The player enters in guest mode, opens a 12–18 minute dinner service, reads a
small reservation book, sets the coal bed and menu, conducts three to five
tables, carves and serves, then closes the house ledger. Early onboarding uses a
shorter 8–12 minute first service with one complete table and recovery branches.

Gameplay is deterministic. Heat, thickness, time, movement, resting, and
sequence produce the result; there is no hidden economic RNG. Failures create
recoverable house decisions rather than deleting scarce items or ending a run.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first playable vertical is a responsive web experience and completes
  without a wallet, faucet, marketplace, token balance, or crypto vocabulary.
- Core verbs are select, temper, rake, sear, turn, move, baste, rest, carve,
  pair, pace, and recover.
- The central capacities are grill zones, rest space, side stations, table
  attention, the butcher case, and the house ledger.
- Doneness and quality are deterministic consequences of an inspectable heat
  path. No random reward controls progression or restaurant economics.
- Profit is an in-game operating result used for repairs, staff care, menu
  chapters, and room restoration. It is non-transferable and has no redemption
  promise.
- Optional onchain work begins only after a complete service and is limited to
  selected keepsakes or provenance records with exact previews and receipts.
- No token, wallet state, owned item, or cross-title asset grants stronger heat
  curves, larger capacities, better guest outcomes, exclusive progression, or
  relief from service pressure.
- Cross-title interoperability is limited to an optional passport, preferences,
  achievement history, provenance receipts, and explicitly mapped cosmetic
  cameos. Currency, inventory, recipes, progression, power, and balance remain
  title-specific.
- MVP chain work, deployment targets, contract architecture, and public-launch
  content breadth are intentionally open decisions until the gameplay vertical
  proves itself.

## Brand Commitments

The name is **The Tender Baron**, pending formal exact and phonetic clearance.
The working line is **A gentle hand on a serious fire.** The Baron is a gracious,
slightly storybook host and mentor—never a cattle magnate, conqueror, butcher
caricature, or macho celebrity chef.

The product shares handcrafted pixel silhouettes, accessibility primitives,
truthful ownership review, and a high craft bar with Dos Esposas and Samurai
Sushi. It must not reuse their palette, page composition, station model, service
tempo, progression, mascot language, or cultural ornament.

## Product Principles

- Fun before wallet.
- Hospitality and craft before financialization.
- Profit as stewardship, never extraction.
- Deterministic firecraft with legible consequences.
- Distinct mechanics inside a coherent family of games.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Every core path supports keyboard, touch, and pointer; coal
raking and item movement require non-drag alternatives. State never depends on
color, motion, sound, or pixel art alone. Reduced motion provides full functional
parity. Text works at 200% zoom, and the first service remains usable at 320 CSS
pixels with 44 CSS pixel minimum targets.
